In the dry season the river drops and the jaguar comes down to the banks to drink, hunt and cool off — the best window in the world to see it in the wild. We set out at dawn and in the late afternoon, reading tracks and movement in the vegetation. The search is active and patient; the sighting, when it comes, is nature deciding — never a promise.
